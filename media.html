Expeditions & Endurance

A visual log of international expeditions, endurance cycling, and global travel. By Richard Massel (Rick Massel).
← Back to Executive Bio Visit Author Site

This gallery documents the athletic endurance and international travel experiences of Richard Massel. Beyond his executive career navigating digital transitions and complex human systems, Richard approaches physical environments with the same strategic resilience.

From organizing multi-day endurance cycling tours across the North American East Coast to summiting high-altitude peaks in Africa, he has explored over 60 countries. These images capture key moments from those global expeditions, long-distance century rides, and remote treks.
