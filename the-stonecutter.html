The Stonecutter

Jacob Riis said, "I look at a stonecutter hammering away at his rock perhaps a hundred times without as much as a crack showing in it."

"Yet at the hundred and first blow it will split in two."

Every effort is weakening the structure of your obstacle. Don't stop hitting the rock.
